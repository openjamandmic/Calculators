Reading the table.
  • Max RPM is the speed at which this pump reaches the filter's rated flow. Cap the pump there, or lower, when using that filter.
  • ANSI-15 fit means the filter's rated flow is above the pressure-pipe limit at 8 FPS and its area meets the media rate.
  • Pump max means the filter is rated above the pump's full-speed flow, so no speed cap is needed for the filter.
